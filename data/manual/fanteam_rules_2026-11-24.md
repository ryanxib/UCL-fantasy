## Definitions (as shown)
- Appearance: every player who competes in the match scores 1.
- 60+ minutes: at least 60 FULL minutes (subbed at 59:30 = no 60+ point and no clean sheet).
- Played full match (MID, FWD): played the full match without being substituted; full match = 90 minutes plus injury time.
- Goal: to the scorer. Own goals count negatively.
- Fantasy assist: final pass to the scorer, unless an opponent makes a clear controlled touch (clearance) first. Deflection = still
  an assist. GK touch in the box generally still gives the assist unless the keeper holds it. No assist if the scorer loses and
  regains possession. Earning a penalty or free kick that is scored = assist (unless the same player takes it). Shot hits
  post/bar and another player scores the rebound = assist. Shot or pass that forces an own goal = assist.
- Caused a scoring free kick: committed the foul that led to a goal direct from the free kick.
- Clean sheet: (definition cut off in screenshots) ... on the pitch for at least 60 minutes.
- Shot on target: would have gone in but for a GK save, or blocked by the last man with the GK beaten. A shot that is a direct
  goal is NOT a shot on target. A shot on target that leads to a foul and a goal still counts. Post or bar is NOT on target.
- Positive / negative impact: whether the player's team won or lost during the time he was on the field.
- Cards: none awarded after the final whistle.

## Safety net (daily games; also some 11-player seasonal games, e.g. Champions League)
- A player who does not start is replaced if possible: same club, same position, cost the same or less; the highest-priced
  eligible player is chosen. Tiebreak: form (average of last 5 games), then season points, then alphabetical.
- If none is found, the original stays; the first available player who comes on is assigned instead.
- No limit on replacements. Starters subbed off are not replaced. Non-starters who come on later ARE replaced.
- Double gameweeks (season games): triggered only for players who started none of their games.
